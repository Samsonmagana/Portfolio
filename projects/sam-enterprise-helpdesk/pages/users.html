Organisational Roles & Responsibilities

Sam Enterprise's six-level organisational access structure.

Owner

The highest authority in Sam Enterprise. Controls company administration, organisational roles and critical system permissions.

Administrator

Manages authorised user accounts, employee administration and approved system operations. Cannot assign or remove the Owner role.

IT Manager

Oversees IT operations, employee information and technical service management.

IT Technician

Investigates technical issues, handles assigned support tickets and assists employees.

Employee

Uses company IT services, reports incidents, submits support requests and accesses authorised self-service resources.

Internal Auditor

Has read-only access to authorised employee and organisational records for independent review and auditing.

Security notice: Employee information, role assignments and account statuses are managed through the Sam Enterprise backend. Server-side authentication and permission checks determine which actions each user is allowed to perform. Only the Owner can assign or remove the Owner role.
